About MotoMetrics

MotoMetrics is a completely offline app designed to seamlessly track and manage both ICE and EV vehicles. It allows you to easily log your fuel usage, EV charging sessions, and maintenance records, as well as track upcoming service reminders. The app features a dedicated savings dashboard to compare energy costs and analyze your total savings. With native Light and Dark modes, all of your data is securely stored 100% locally on your device, ensuring your vehicle metrics remain entirely private.
