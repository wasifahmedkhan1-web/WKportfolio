About Budgetry

Budgetry is a smart, student- and youth-focused expense tracker designed to simplify personal finance in India. Effortlessly manage your monthly allowance, lock away recurring fixed bills upfront (like rent, PG, or mess fees), and track your "Safe-to-Spend" daily balance so you never accidentally overspend.

Core Features

Allowance & Income Setup

Configure your monthly income or student allowance with flexible cycle dates to match your exact financial timeline.

Locked Fixed Bills

Securely lock away recurring fixed expenses like rent, PG, mess, or tuition upfront so that money stays protected from accidental spending.

Safe-to-Spend Balance

Real-time tracking of your flexible pool and "Safe Today's Limit" calculation to ensure your daily spending stays completely guilt-free and on track.

Expense & History Tracking

Easily log daily expenses and review your spending pacing to keep your budget safe throughout the entire month cycle.
