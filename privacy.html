Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Pune, India

1. Introduction

I value your privacy and believe in building software with complete transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is treated across this portfolio website (wasifahmedkhan1-web.github.io/WKportfolio) and the mobile applications developed by Wasif Ahmed Khan.

2. Portfolio Website Data Collection

This website is an informational developer portfolio designed to showcase projects and facilitate direct communication:

  • Contact Form: When you send a message through the contact form, the details you submit (name, email address, message body) are used strictly to reply to your inquiry. Data is submitted directly through encrypted form endpoints (e.g., Web3Forms) or your default mail client.
  • No Tracking Cookies: This website does not deploy advertising trackers or intrusive third-party profiling cookies. Browser localStorage is used solely to store your preferred visual theme (dark or light mode).

3. Mobile Applications Disclosures

I develop Android applications with a privacy-first mindset. Below are the specific policies for individual published apps:

Navixo (Location Sharing)

Navixo is designed for trusted family and friend circle safety.

  • Location Data: Navixo accesses your device's foreground and background location only when you explicitly enable active sharing sessions or emergency alerts.
  • No Third-Party Sale: Your coordinates are never sold, monetized, or shared with advertisers.
  • Granular Control: Users can toggle Incognito/Ghost mode or terminate active sharing at any time.
  • Map Providers: Map tiles are rendered using OpenStreetMap services.

MotoMetrics & Budgetry

Both MotoMetrics (automotive fuel & EV analytics) and Budgetry (allowance and expense tracker) prioritize local data residency:

  • All logs, transactions, expenses, and vehicle records are stored locally on your device storage (SQLite / Room database).
  • No financial data or telemetry is transmitted to external proprietary servers.

Digital Tasbih

Digital Tasbih is a completely offline, distraction-free tool. It requests zero internet permissions, contains no advertisements, and stores only your counting preferences locally.

4. Data Security

Standard administrative and technical protections are maintained to safeguard any communication transmitted through this site. However, no internet transmission is 100% immune from vulnerabilities.
